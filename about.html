about us

We got tired of
five tabs at 2am

Terminus started as an internal tool at a fintech startup, built by three SREs who were done correlating logs, metrics, and alerts across five different dashboards during an outage.

our mission

Give every engineer root-cause clarity, not more dashboards

Most observability tools are built for the people who buy them, not the people who use them at 2am. We flipped that: every feature ships after being pressure-tested by our own on-call rotation first.

Today Terminus is used by engineering teams from 3-person startups to 400-engineer scale-ups, all trusting us to be the first tool they open when something's on fire.

how we work

The principles behind the product

◆

Dogfood everything

Terminus monitors Terminus. If a feature isn't good enough for our own on-call rotation, it doesn't ship.

⚑

Fast beats feature-complete

A 200ms search you'll actually use beats a powerful one that takes ten seconds to load during an incident.

◨

Default to transparency

Public status page, public roadmap, public incident postmortems — for us and for the outages our tool helps you catch.
